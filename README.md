# Algebraic phylogenetics, in Lean

[![CI](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-algebraic/actions/workflows/ci.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-algebraic/actions/workflows/ci.yml)

Lean 4 formalizations of algebraic phylogenetics, one paper per repository: the polynomial structure
of the models of sequence evolution on trees, and what it says about the trees and parameters that
can be recovered from data. They start with phylogenetic invariants and the Fourier transform of
group-based models; continue with the identifiability of tree topologies and of the parameters of
substitution, rate and indel models; then mixtures of trees and their nonidentifiability; and end
with the reconstruction of trees by spectral and algebraic methods.

This repository is their hub, in the collection rooted at
[lean4-phylogenetics](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics). It holds:

- the library [`PhyloAlgebraic`](PhyloAlgebraic), for what two papers or more of this hub share and the root library
  does not hold. It has nothing yet: what they share so far (the trees on which the models are defined, and generic parameters: null sets of parameters and the zero sets of analytic functions) is in the root library.
  Its [README](PhyloAlgebraic/README.md) says what goes there;
- the catalog of the papers, [`catalog/papers.toml`](catalog/papers.toml), from which the tables
  below are generated.

The [conventions](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/main/docs/CONVENTIONS.md) that
every repository follows, the [workflow](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/main/docs/WORKFLOW.md),
the registry of the repositories, the shared foundations and the tools are in the root. Each paper is
formalized with the [formalize-math-paper](https://github.com/vltanh/formalize-math-paper) skill:
every result the paper proves is proved by the paper's own argument, the statements are checked
against the paper, and an audit of the paper reports its errors and gaps.

## The papers

Each paper is in one family, the one its question belongs to. This is a hub of theory: general
results on the models of sequence evolution are here, and a result about one estimator is with that
estimator, in a hub of inference
([conventions](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics/blob/main/docs/CONVENTIONS.md), Section
1). SVDquartets, whose results concern inference under the multispecies coalescent, is in
[lean4-phylo-species-tree](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylo-species-tree); the algebra it rests
on is here. *Uses* lists the papers, of any hub, that the paper's proofs are known to use, and the
other hubs whose library it requires. Each paper's inventory completes the list.

## Starting a paper

From [lean4-phylogenetics](https://github.com/vltanh/lean4-phylogenetics), beside this repository,
when the paper's formalization starts:

```sh
python3 scripts/status.py next          # the open papers, by priority, with what each waits on
python3 scripts/new_paper.py steel1994  # creates ../lean4-steel1994 on the train, under this hub
cd ../lean4-steel1994 && claude         # then: /formalize-math-paper https://doi.org/10.1016/0893-9659(94)90024-8
```

## Building

```sh
lake exe cache get
lake build
lake build Phylogenetics
lake env lean scripts/Audit.lean
```

Lean and Mathlib `v4.37.0-rc1`; [`lakefile.toml`](lakefile.toml) requires lean4-phylogenetics by
commit, `3fcabfd`. The audit, [`scripts/Audit.lean`](scripts/Audit.lean), checks that every
declaration of this library and of lean4-phylogenetics depends only on Lean's standard axioms
`propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`; `python3 scripts/gen_audit.py` generates its
`import all` lines. CI runs the build and the audit, and checks that the tables above are current.
